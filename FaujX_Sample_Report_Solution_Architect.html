What to Probe Live

Gaps found before the interview — so your panel spends its time where the risk is.

Cloud-native vs. middleware default

Tended to reach for an ESB-style pattern where a lighter, event-driven or container-based design would fit better.

Ask: “Redesign this flow without the service bus. What do you lose and what do you gain?”

Payments security and regulation

Named the right controls in general terms but was thin on how regulatory needs shape the integration design itself.

Ask: “Walk me through the audit trail and data boundaries for a payment moving through this design.”

Cost of running the target state

Strong on migration steps, lighter on the ongoing run-cost of the end state.

Ask: “Which part of your target design gets most expensive at 10x volume, and what would you change?”
