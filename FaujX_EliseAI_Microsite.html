Hiring funnel · the industry default

Resume screen discards everything but keywords. Recruiter screen starts from zero. Technical screen starts from zero again. Onsite re-asks what three stages already covered.

Every stage throws away the signal of the stage before it. The candidate re-introduces themselves four times. The biggest leak isn't at the top of this funnel — it's at every handoff.

FHOS is your continuity principle applied to hiring. Signal carries forward across every evaluation stage — gaps become targeted probes instead of silent rejections — and the funnel ends in one decision-ready capability report before any interview time is spent.

Your recruiters build assessments by hand

EliseAI's own recruiter posting asks the team to own creation of job descriptions and practical assessments per role. That craftsmanship is exactly right at 20 roles. At hundreds of roles spanning staff engineers to strategic AEs to a CMO, hand-built evaluation is the first system that breaks — quality varies by author, calibration drifts by week, and the backlog lands on a recruiting function you're still building (the Director of Talent search is live).

What FHOS changes: a role benchmark is generated from your live posting in minutes, then adaptive screening, role challenges, and structured interviews are derived from it — consistent across every role, configurable by your hiring managers at five human-in-the-loop checkpoints.
Source: EliseAI recruiter and Director of Talent postings, 2026

Every stage forgets the last one

The standard hiring stack ranks resumes; it doesn't carry evaluation forward. A gap spotted at resume screen becomes a rejection instead of a question. A strength shown in the technical screen never reaches the onsite panel. Candidates repeat themselves to four different evaluators — the precise anti-pattern your Zillow case study quantified in the leasing funnel.

What FHOS changes: the non-discarding principle. Gaps carry forward as targeted probes — "no direct LLM-serving experience" becomes a structured interview question, not a silent filter — and every score in the final report traces to evidence from the full journey.
Source: FHOS pipeline architecture · see sample reports below

Your builders' hours are the scarcest input

At >100% growth, every interview hour comes out of shipping. Staff-level engineering loops alone consume multiple senior engineers per candidate — multiplied across hundreds of roles, hiring quietly becomes one of the largest engineering taxes in the company, and the people paying it are the ones you hired to build product.

What FHOS changes: roughly 80% of the hire/no-hire signal is delivered before a single interviewer hour is spent. Your team's time goes to the final-loop judgment calls the report flags — with a precise agenda instead of a blank hour.
