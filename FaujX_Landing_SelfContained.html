Hiring isn't broken because the tools are bad. It's broken because the system has no signal.

Your ATS tracks applicants. Your recruiters match keywords. Your team runs interview loops. None of it measures whether the candidate can actually do the job. That gap — visible in every geography, every function, every level — is where mis-hires get born.

A · Time destruction

Internal hours burned on candidates who never had a realistic shot. Shortlists keyword-matched, not capability-matched. Interview coordination eating 30–40% of hiring-team calendars.

FHOS fix: eliminate unqualified candidates before any internal time is invested.

B · No signal

What "ready to hire" means depends on who's interviewing that week. No structured framework, no pre-interview signal, no post-hire data. That isn't a bar — that's variance.

FHOS fix: structured, comparable, multi-dimensional capability signal.

C · Cost of getting it wrong

Mis-hires surface 4–6 months in, after the cost is burned. No audit trail means legal exposure on every hire. Recruiter fees of 20–25% arrive with no validation included.

FHOS fix: pre-interview validation that dramatically reduces the probability of a wrong hire.

07 · Queries

The questions every hiring leader asks first.

Does FHOS replace my ATS?

No. FHOS sits above or alongside Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, or a custom HRMS via REST APIs, webhooks, and batch sync. Your workflows stay intact — FHOS adds the capability signal they're missing.

Who makes the final decision — AI or humans?

Humans, always. Zero automated rejections. Every shortlist passes through a domain-expert reviewer with full override authority and an audit trail. The system informs; your team decides.

Which roles and levels does it cover?

Engineering, Sales & GTM, Product, Operations, and People — from campus and fresher hiring through Director, VP, and CXO. The architecture is the same; the benchmark is calibrated to each role and level. Our deepest case-study proof to date is in engineering.

How is our data handled?

Three deployment models: white-label SaaS with encrypted tenant separation (live in days), dedicated cloud instance with configurable data residency (1–2 weeks), or fully sovereign enterprise VPC where data never leaves your infrastructure (2–4 weeks). EEO, GDPR, and ADA compliance included; SOC 2 configurable.

Pilot pricing scales with scope — role count and candidate volume — and is structured to sit under VP-discretionary spend, no procurement committee required. Every pilot is paid; pilot credit applies toward an enterprise contract. Email us for the breakdown in your market currency.
