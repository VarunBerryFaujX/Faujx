The Staff bar for this role

Derived from the posted responsibilities, not a generic data-engineer template. Gold markers are execution capabilities a strong Senior IC will usually clear. Green markers are the Staff-defining capabilities — architecture, standardization, and cross-team influence — where the loop is most often spent, and most often wasted.
At Staff level for an Analytics Data Engineering role, "can they build reliable pipelines" is table stakes. The role is defined by whether they can architect a shared semantic layer as a single source of truth, drive modeling and CI/CD standards across teams, design shift-left governance (data contracts, SLOs, code-enforced quality gates), and translate ambiguous metric definitions into certified pipelines that executives trust. That is what separates a Staff hire from an excellent Senior one — and it is exactly what a coding-heavy first round tends not to surface.
